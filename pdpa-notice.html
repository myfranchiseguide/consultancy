Personal Data Protection Notice.

How MYFranchiseGuide collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data, in accordance with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

MYFranchiseGuide ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the personal data of everyone who visits this website, submits an enquiry, attends our events, joins a training programme, or otherwise engages with our services. This notice is issued in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 ("PDPA") of Malaysia and explains how your personal data is collected, used, disclosed and protected.

2. Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Identification details such as your name, email address and phone number;
  • Business details such as your company name and the nature of your enquiry (e.g. brand expansion, business investment, partnership);
  • Information you provide through our enquiry form, event registration forms, or WhatsApp/social media messages;
  • Technical data such as browser type and general usage data collected automatically when you browse this website.

3. Purpose of Processing

We collect and use your personal data to:

  • Respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form or other channels;
  • Register and manage your participation in MYFG Academy events, workshops and training programmes (including TFI Brand Edition, TFI v2, The Franchise Insider and Personal Branding Academy);
  • Provide franchise advisory, business expansion and partnership-related services;
  • Send relevant updates about our programmes, events and services, where you have consented to receive them;
  • Comply with any legal or regulatory obligations.

4. Disclosure of Personal Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted third-party service providers who support our operations (such as form, email and event-registration platforms) strictly for the purposes stated above, and only to the extent necessary. We may also disclose personal data where required by law or by a competent authority.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure or alteration. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes it was collected for, or as required by law, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights

Under the PDPA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated personal data;
  • Withdraw your consent to our processing of your personal data at any time;
  • Limit the processing of your personal data for certain purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 9 below.

8. Changes to This Notice

We may update this notice from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this notice, or wish to exercise your rights under the PDPA, please contact us through our website or message us on WhatsApp.
