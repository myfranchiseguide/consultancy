2.Personal data we collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Your business details, such as company or brand name, job title and the nature of your enquiry.
  • Information you give us when you register for an event, fill in a form, or message us on WhatsApp or social media.
  • Basic technical data when you visit our website, such as browser type and pages visited.

We collect this data directly from you, or from ticketing and registration platforms you use to sign up for our events.

3.Why we use it

  • To register you for, and manage your participation in, our events, workshops and programmes, including The Franchise Insider.
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide franchise advisory, expansion and partnership services.
  • To send you updates about our programmes and events, where you have agreed to receive them.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

4.Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Event organisers and partners of the specific event you register for, including co-organisers, title partners and sponsors. We may share your name, contact details and company details with them, and they may contact you about the event and their products, services and events.
  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as ticketing, payment, form, email and event platforms.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5.Transfers outside Malaysia

Some of our service providers and event partners may store or process data outside Malaysia. When this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your data is protected to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

6.Is giving us your data required?

Your name, email address and phone number are required to register for our events and to respond to you. If you do not provide them, we may not be able to register you or reply to your enquiry. All other information is optional.

7.Your rights

  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.
  • Withdraw your consent, or ask us to limit how we use your data, at any time, including stopping marketing messages.

To make a request, email myfranchiseguide@gmail.com with the subject "PDPA Request". We will respond within 21 days. Withdrawing consent does not affect data already shared before your request, and may mean we can no longer provide some services to you.

8.How long we keep it

We keep your personal data for up to 24 months after your last event or interaction with us, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. After that, we securely delete or anonymise it.

9.How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. If a data breach occurs that is likely to cause you significant harm, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

10.Changes to this Notice

We may update this Notice from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

This Notice is issued in English and Bahasa Malaysia. If there is any inconsistency between the two versions, the English version prevails.
