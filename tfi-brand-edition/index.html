Who it's for

Built for franchise brands, and the businesses ready to become one

This is for you if

  • You're a founder, CEO, COO or head of franchise development at a franchise brand
  • You run an F&B, retail, education, services or wellness brand, from newly registered to established
  • You're planning to expand your business through the franchise model
  • You're a business owner curious whether franchising is right for you
  • You're C-suite and ready to be challenged by peers who've been where you are

This is not for you if

  • You're only shopping for a franchise to buy. Our community editions are built for investors and future franchisees.
  • You're a supplier hoping to sell to the room. Partner places are by invitation only.
  • You want a lecture you can sit through quietly. You'll be asked to share.
  • You're not open to discussing real numbers and real mistakes with peers
